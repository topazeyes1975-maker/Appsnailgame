MAIN BASE COMMAND
Astral Knight
PLAYER CHARACTER
Navigate the maze using the touch joystick or WASD keys. Avoid hazards and reach all stranded babies.
Galactic Baby
RESCUE TARGET
Locate and pick up all 3 lost babies in each maze to unlock safety and clear the level mission.
Nebula Vanguard
PATROL ENEMY
Patrols the corridors and fires red laser blasts when in line of sight. Contact or blasts deplete health.
Neon Liberty Bell
STUN HAZARD
Bounces unpredictably off walls. Touching this bell freezes your movement controls for 2.5 seconds.
